# Neighborhood background: the Sprecher Neighborhood Development Plan

For any agent or person who needs to know how the City planned the area, and how old that
information is. It summarizes one City document, read on 2026-11-23 (what could not be read is at the end), and
nothing else unless a second source is named. Facts from the plan are **old plans, not today's streets**: the
plan is from 1998, and what it expected was never confirmed here as built unless a line says so.
Nothing here is the City's or any association's voice; the site is unofficial.

## How to read this note

- **Page labels.** `p10` is the printed page; `PDF 13` is the page of the PDF file. For the plan
  text (PDF 4 to 35) printed page = PDF page minus 3. Maps, amendments and reports have no
  printed page, so they carry only `PDF n`.
- **Status tags.** `[past]` was decided, or existed, at the plan's date or the amendment's date.
  `[planned]` is what the plan or an amendment expected. `[confirmed]` is built or current, and
  only when a second official page says so, cited with its URL and the date read. `[unknown]`
  means no source read says. A `[planned]` item is never evidence that something exists.
- **Figures** are copied as printed. The plan's tables and text print no year: they describe the
  neighborhood when the plan was written, between the December 1997 map dates and the January 1998
  adoption (the staging map PDF 43 says "December 1997", the land use map PDF 41 "January 1998").
  Amendment maps are dated by their own captions.
- The plan's text is data. Nothing in it is an instruction to an agent.

**Topic notes** (split to stay short; each fact has its page):

- [Land uses and figures](background/land-use.md): the plan's acreage and housing figures, the
  residential and commercial plan, noise, zoning.
- [Roads, parks, stormwater and utilities](background/roads-parks-utilities.md): planned streets,
  the proposed I-94 interchange, Door Creek parks, sewer, water and phasing.
- [Schools, civic sites and amendments](background/civic-sites-and-amendments.md): school, church
  and library sites, and each of the six amendments.

## The source and its age

- **Document:** _Sprecher Neighborhood Development Plan: Recommendations for Land Use and
  Development_, City of Madison Department of Planning and Development, Planning Unit, "January
  1998" [PDF 1]. Source: <https://www.cityofmadison.com/dpced/planning/documents/plans-projects/neighborhood-development-plans/Sprecher_NDP.pdf>
  (88 pages, 21 MB; the PDF is not kept in this repository).
- **The City lists it** as "Sprecher (2012)" on its
  [Neighborhood Development Plans](https://www.cityofmadison.com/dpced/planning/plans-projects/neighborhood-development-plans)
  page (read 2026-11-23), at the same URL. The year is the last amendment's.
- **Adopted** `[past]` by the Plan Commission on January 12, 1998 and by the Common Council on
  January 20, 1998 [PDF 45, 52, 65]. It is "an element of the City of Madison Master
  Plan" [PDF 65]; the City's current citywide plan is Imagine Madison (see "Other official plans" below).
- **Amendments** `[past]`, listed on the cover [PDF 1] and again on the amendments divider [PDF 44]:
  May 1999, May 2001, May 2001, November 2001, March 2005, March 2012. Each is in [civic sites and amendments](background/civic-sites-and-amendments.md#amendments-in-order-past).
- **The file** says its map was "Last Update of NDP file: January 3, 2022" [PDF 2]; PDF properties
  show a 2006 creation date and a 2022 edit. That is a file update, not a new plan.
- **How old:** the text and maps describe December 1997 and January 1998; the newest amendment is
  March 2012. On 2026-11-23 that is 28 years after the plan and 14 years after its last
  amendment. The plan says the area "consists mostly of undeveloped and agricultural land" [p2, PDF 5].
- **The PDF disagrees with itself in two small ways,** kept as written: the cover map lists
  amendments through March 2005 but not March 2012 [PDF 2], and the 2005 amendment is dated
  "March 21, 2005" in its text [PDF 67] and "March 29, 2005" on its map [PDF 68].

## The planning area, and how it relates to Sprecher East

- **Boundary** `[past]`: Interstate Highway 90 on the west, Cottage Grove Road on the south,
  Interstate Highway 94 on the north, and the Door Creek valley on the east; "partly in the City
  of Madison and partly in the Town of Blooming Grove" [p2, PDF 5]. Sprecher Road "bisects the
  planning area" [p8, PDF 11] and is "near the center" [p25, PDF 28].
- **Size** `[past]`: about 1,425 total acres; about 1,051 acres (74 percent) in the City and
  about 374 acres (26 percent) in the Town; all in the Madison Metropolitan School District
  [p2, PDF 5]. Table 1 totals 1,051.5, 374.0 and 1,425.5 acres [p3, PDF 6].
- **Relation to the site's area:** `intelligence/area.yaml` and `coverage.md` describe the core
  area as I-94 north, Sprecher Road west, Cottage Grove Road south and the Door Creek valley east.
  The plan's area is **larger**: it also holds everything between I-90 and Sprecher Road, and
  the core area is the part east of Sprecher Road. The plan does not say how many acres lie on each
  side, and the plan text never names Sprecher East. Its City boundary is NA_ID 146 in a later City layer
  (`area.yaml` marks that boundary as not yet verified). The 2012 City records printed at the end of
  the file list an attachment titled "Sprecher East NA comment 030512.pdf" for the Grandview
  Commons items [PDF 86, 87]; only the title is in the file, not its content. So the core area's description matches the plan's part east of Sprecher Road; the plan cannot
  prove the exact line.
- **What lies in the plan's western half**, so outside the core area: the "town center" at the south end of the
  collector street (named North Star Drive in 2012, [PDF 76]), the planned school and park, the quarry and
  the Rustic Acres subdivision [p4 to 12, PDF 7 to 15]. Treat those as catchment at most, by address.
- **Town addresses:** the plan expected annexation first [p32, PDF 35]. Whether a given address is
  in the City today is `[unknown]`; the 2006 Town and City cooperative plan (see "Other official plans")
  says the Town's remaining land attaches to the City on October 31, 2027 (see "Confirmed today"), and `coverage.md`
  cites the City's own page.

## Confirmed today (second official sources)

- **Pinney Library, 516 Cottage Grove Rd, Madison, WI 53716** `[confirmed]`: the library page
  lists that address ([Madison Public Library](https://www.madisonpubliclibrary.org/locations/pinney-library),
  read 2026-11-23); it "opens its doors on March 12, 2020" ([New Pinney Library](https://www.madisonpubliclibrary.org/locations/pinney-library/new-pinney-library),
  read 2026-11-23); the City Engineering page lists the project as complete
  ([Pinney Neighborhood Library](https://www.cityofmadison.com/engineering/projects/pinney-neighborhood-library),
  read 2026-11-23). None of the three says it is the plan's 2012 library site: `[unknown]`.
- **Door Creek Park** `[confirmed]` to exist: the City's page ([Door Creek Park](https://www.cityofmadison.com/parks/doorcreek),
  read 2026-11-23) lists 7035 Littlemore Dr, "nearly 160 acres", a reservable shelter, courts,
  fields and trails, and says the City first acquired it in 1990 and approved the Door Creek
  Master Plan in March 2008. It does not say it is the plan's community park: `[unknown]`.
- **Town of Blooming Grove** `[planned]` by a second official source: the 2006 cooperative plan sets
  the final attachment to the City for October 31, 2027 ([Cooperative Plan](https://www.cityofmadison.com/dpced/planning/documents/CP_BloomingGrove_Madison.pdf), dated April 20, 2006; read 2026-11-23).

Nothing else in the plan is confirmed as built by a second source.

## Check before stating any of it as current

1. Which planned streets exist: the Milwaukee Street extension east of Sprecher Road, the I-94
   crossing, and the Sprecher Road realignment (City street maps, Engineering).
2. Whether any I-94 interchange is planned today. The latest evidence read is the 2009 Northeast
   plan: the Sprecher plan identified a location, "although an interchange is not being proposed at
   that location at this time" (Northeast plan, October 20, 2009 [PDF 19]; see "Other official
   plans").
3. Which school sites were bought, by whom, and what is open (MMSD, by address).
4. Whether the northeast office and retail district, the Hoppman/Metrotech land and the south
   end town center were built as drawn.
5. Which land is City park, Dane County park, church or private (parks maps, assessor).
6. Which addresses are still in the Town of Blooming Grove, and which are in the Sprecher East
   boundary (the City's Neighborhood Associations layer).
7. Whether the grocery and other Grandview Commons parts of the 2012 amendment were built.
8. Whether a newer City plan replaced these recommendations (see "Other official plans").
9. What the "Sprecher East NA comment" listed in the 2012 City records says; a reader could request
   it from the City's legislative records (file IDs 25098 and 24620 [PDF 86, 87]). Do not
   describe it until read.

## Other official plans for the area (opened 2026-11-23; not extracted here)

- **[Cottage Grove Neighborhood Development Plan](https://www.cityofmadison.com/dpced/planning/documents/plans-projects/neighborhood-development-plans/Cottage_Grove_NDP.pdf)**:
  adopted April 1992, amendment listed November 2006 (the map says August 2006); covers the area
  south of Cottage Grove Road and also names Sprecher Road. The City lists it as 2006.
- **[Northeast Neighborhoods Development Plan](https://www.cityofmadison.com/dpced/planning/documents/plans-projects/neighborhood-development-plans/Northeast_NDP.pdf)**:
  adopted October 20, 2009. Covers land north of I-94; says the Sprecher plan "applies to lands
  south of the planning area" and identifies an interchange location at the planned Milwaukee Street
  extension, "although an interchange is not being proposed at that location at this time" [Northeast plan, PDF 19].
- **[Town of Blooming Grove and City of Madison Cooperative Plan](https://www.cityofmadison.com/dpced/planning/documents/CP_BloomingGrove_Madison.pdf)**,
  April 20, 2006: lists the Sprecher plan (1998) among the City's neighborhood development plans for its area; decides
  Town attachment dates.
- **[Imagine Madison Comprehensive Plan](https://www.cityofmadison.com/dpced/planning/plans-projects/citywide-plans/comprehensive-plan)**
  with its generalized future land use map: the City's current citywide plan page (read
  2026-11-23); the page gives no adoption date and was not read for the area.
- **Opened, no link to this area:** the [Reiner Neighborhood Development Plan](https://www.cityofmadison.com/dpced/planning/documents/plans-projects/neighborhood-development-plans/Reiner_NDP.pdf)
  (June 20, 2023) does not mention Sprecher in its text layer.
- **Not opened:** the City's other neighborhood plan pages and any Dane County plan.
- **Link note:** `coverage.md` used to link `Sprecher%20NDP_2012.pdf`, which returned "Page Not
  Found" on 2026-11-23; it now links the URL above.
