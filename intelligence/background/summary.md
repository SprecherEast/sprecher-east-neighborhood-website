# Neighborhood background: summary

What the City planned for the area, in a few lines. Every fact is from the City's Sprecher
Neighborhood Development Plan, with its page, in [`background.md`](../background.md) and the topic
notes. Read it for context, never as today's facts.

- The plan was adopted in January 1998 and last amended in March 2012. It is old: 28 years in 2026. It describes an area that was "mostly undeveloped and agricultural land".
- It expected, and nothing here confirms built: a street grid, the Milwaukee Street extension east
  across the Door Creek corridor, Sprecher Road widened and realigned, a second crossing under I-94,
  parks and school sites, a town center, and shops and offices. Planned is never built.
- An I-94 interchange east of Sprecher Road was only "suggested", with "no commitment"; a 2009 plan
  says none was being proposed.
- Its area is larger than Sprecher East: both sides of Sprecher Road, from I-90 to the Door Creek
  valley. The school, park and town center it draws are mostly west of Sprecher Road. A place in
  the plan still gets its tier by address.
- Confirmed by a second City page: Pinney Library at 516 Cottage Grove Rd (open since March 2020),
  and Door Creek Park (about 160 acres, 7035 Littlemore Dr). The Town of Blooming Grove's remaining
  land is set to join the City on October 31, 2027.
- Say "the plan expected" with its year. Never state a plan figure as today's.
