# Background: roads, parks, stormwater and utilities

Planned streets, parks and open space, stormwater, sewer, water and phasing. Page labels and status tags (`[past]`, `[planned]`, `[confirmed]`, `[unknown]`) are explained in the
[index](../background.md#how-to-read-this-note). The plan is from 1998: everything marked `[planned]` is
what the City expected, not what exists.

## Roads and connections `[planned]`

- **Three existing arterials** `[past]`: Cottage Grove Road, Sprecher Road and Milwaukee Street
  [p9, PDF 12]. Planned right-of-way is 120 feet for all three, 80 feet for the two collectors, and
  60 feet for most local streets [p21 to 23, PDF 24 to 26].
- **Cottage Grove Road** was expected to become "four travel lanes and a median" [p21, PDF 24].
  **Milwaukee Street** was expected to extend east across the Door Creek corridor to Gaston Road
  [p21, PDF 24]. **Sprecher Road** was expected to need "at least four travel lanes" and to be
  realigned east, starting "about one-third of a mile north" of Cottage Grove Road [p22, PDF 25].
- **A second crossing under I-94** between Sprecher and Gaston Roads, for the neighborhood to
  the north [p21, PDF 24].
- **An I-94 interchange** was "suggested" about "three-quarters of a mile east of Sprecher Road";
  it was "not currently a recommendation of the regional transportation plan" and the plan records
  "no commitment" by any government [p22, PDF 25]. `[unknown]` today.
- **Street pattern:** "grid-like", with sidewalks on all public streets, collector and local
  streets also serving as bikeways, and two wide boulevards: an east-west one with a 100-foot
  drainage median and a north-south one with a 75-foot median over a gas pipeline easement
  [p9, PDF 12; p23, PDF 26].
- **Buses:** a Madison Metro route was drawn, but "public bus service is unlikely to be extended
  to the Sprecher Neighborhood initially" [p23, PDF 26]. `[unknown]` today.
- **Trails:** a regional trail in the Door Creek corridor was proposed, to link to a Capital City
  State Trail extension about one mile south of Cottage Grove Road [p23 to 24, PDF 26 to 27].

## Parks, open space and stormwater `[planned]`

- **Parks:** five neighborhood parks of 4 to 6 acres each, plus an 11-acre area park inside a
  22-acre combined school and park site; 34.8 acres in all [p18, PDF 21]. A small overlook over the
  Door Creek lowlands was proposed [p18, PDF 21].
- **Door Creek corridor:** a City community park (picnic shelters, softball diamonds) in the
  western part, athletic fields to the north, and a Dane County conservation and recreation
  corridor with hiking and cross-country ski trails in the east; the plan calls the idea "still
  evolving" and says City and County parks departments must coordinate [p19, PDF 22; p32, PDF 35].
  A long-range hope was trails along the whole corridor "between Sun Prairie and the Village of
  McFarland" [p19, PDF 22]. The May 2001 amendment adds 32 acres of park and open space and a Dane
  County commitment to buy that land [PDF 50].
- **Gateway:** keep the two low hills either side of I-94 at Door Creek, and the corridor, as open
  space, to mark an edge to the city [p20, PDF 23].
- **Stormwater:** a 100-foot-wide greenway and detention area from near Rustic Acres east to Door
  Creek; a detention area in the southeast corner; and one on the Astronautics land by I-90 [p19 to
  20, PDF 22 to 23]. Whether the boulevard-median design "can be successfully combined" was
  "not certain" [p19, PDF 22].

## Utilities and phasing `[planned]`

- **When the plan was written** `[past]`: a 138-kV electric line on the west side of Sprecher Road in a 110-foot
  easement, and two ANR natural gas pipelines in 75-foot and 50-foot easements [p4, PDF 7].
- **Sewer:** most of the area drains east to a new interceptor in the Door Creek corridor; its
  construction was to "begin in 1998" [p26, PDF 29]. A small portion of the neighborhood could drain west or
  south to existing City sewers [p25, PDF 28]. **Water:** a new City reservoir near the school site, "expected to be built during
  1999" [p27, PDF 30]. Whether either was built is `[unknown]`.
- **Staging map** (Areas A to E; [PDF 43]): development "allowed to begin" in Area A (southwest) in
  1998, Area B (southeast) in 1999 and Area C (northeast) in 2000, "subject to the availability of all
  necessary public infrastructure"; Areas D and E "at a later time" [p28, PDF 31]. Area C holds
  the Milwaukee Street extension [p27, PDF 30].
- **Process steps the plan asked for** `[planned]`: add the land to the Central Urban Service
  Area, update the Official Map, budget public works, annex before urban development, and
  cooperate with Dane County Parks [p29 to 32, PDF 32 to 35].
