# Background: schools, civic sites and amendments

Planned school, church and civic sites, and each amendment to the plan. Page labels and status tags (`[past]`, `[planned]`, `[confirmed]`, `[unknown]`) are explained in the
[index](../background.md#how-to-read-this-note). The plan is from 1998: everything marked `[planned]` is
what the City expected, not what exists.

## Schools and civic sites

- **School and park site** `[planned]`: 22 acres combined near the center of the western half;
  Madison Metropolitan School District staff "are satisfied with the location and size"; the plan
  says an additional school east of the interstate is "very possible" but "not certain"
  [p17, PDF 20]. Do not say which school serves an address; use MMSD's tool (`coverage.md`).
- **Church site** `[past]`/`[planned]`: a 32-acre institutional site owned or being bought by the
  Buckeye Evangelical Free Church [p17, PDF 20]. The May 2001 amendments add a 20-acre site for St.
  Matthew's Lutheran Church [PDF 50] and an alternative school site of about 24 acres east of
  Sprecher Road, bounded by Sprecher Road, Dominion Drive, Wyalusing Drive and a proposed park, with the
  western site still "the preferred location" [PDF 52 to 54]. Which parcel each organization
  ended up with is `[unknown]`.
- **Doric Lodge** (nearly six acres, `[past]`): kept institutional by the plan [p17, PDF 20]; the
  2012 amendment folds most of the site into the town center [PDF 70].
- **Two illustrative community-center sites** next to the two commercial centers [p18, PDF 21].
- **Library** `[planned]`: the 2012 amendment shows a branch library site south of Sharpsburg
  Drive [PDF 70], and a February 2012 City report says the library was "included in the Capital
  Improvement Program for design in 2014 and construction in 2015" [PDF 79].

- **March 2012 evidence.** The amendment text in the file is a draft with "\_\_, 2012" [PDF 69]. The
  City's February 16, 2012 report recommended approval and described a proposed grocery of "58,000
  square foot" [PDF 74, 85]. The related comprehensive-plan resolution passed the Plan Commission
  on March 5, 2012 [PDF 86]. The rezoning ordinance for the same project was adopted by the
  Common Council on March 20, 2012 by 15 to 4 and enacted March 29, 2012 [PDF 87 to 88]; it covers
  "109,000 sq. ft. of future retail/office, a 24,000 sq. ft. library, 110 multi-family units" and an
  18-lot single-family subdivision [PDF 87]. **The file holds no record of the vote on the plan
  amendment itself**; the cover says March 2012 [PDF 1]. Treat March 2012 as the adoption month,
  `[unknown]` for the day.
