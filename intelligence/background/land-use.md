[index](../background.md#how-to-read-this-note). The plan is from 1998: everything marked `[planned]` is
what the City expected, not what exists.

## Land uses `[planned]`

- **Residential** `[planned]`: "primarily a residential community" with a range of housing types,
  densities and tenure; most medium and low-medium density near commercial centers or major parks
  [p10, PDF 13; p8, PDF 11]. Mapped densities are "illustrative" with "some flexibility" [p10, PDF 13].
- **East of Sprecher Road** `[planned]`: medium and low-medium density south of an extended
  Milwaukee Street across from the office district; medium density around a central open space where
  three boulevard streets meet; medium and low-medium in the southeast corner by the Door Creek park
  [p13, PDF 16].
- **Two commercial centers** `[planned]`, with "no regional retailing or major highway-oriented
  uses" in either [p8, PDF 11]:
  - A larger **northeast office and retail district** east of Sprecher Road between I-94 and a
    Milwaukee Street extension: offices on the north, a community shopping and service center on
    the south (a large grocery, a pharmacy and the like are listed as examples), and a small
    convenience center south of Milwaukee Street [p14, PDF 17]. The plan warned "it may be several
    years before there is a large enough local market" [p14, PDF 17].
  - A smaller **southwest "town center"** at the south end of the western collector street, on land
    then part of the Astronautics Corporation Technology Center, planned as a walkable,
    multi-story, mixed-use street with parking behind buildings [p15, PDF 18]. It needed "a more
    detailed development plan and design guidelines" first [p29 to 30, PDF 32 to 33]; the plan noted "no
    indication" that the owner was considering redevelopment [p16, PDF 19].
- **One other commercial spot** `[planned]`: a small parcel at the northwest corner of Sprecher
  and Cottage Grove Roads, for a limited highway-oriented use such as a service station or
  convenience store, or a civic use such as a fire or precinct station [p17, PDF 20].
- **Highway noise** `[planned]`: housing next to I-90 and I-94 must show sound levels are
  permissible or be attenuated; berms "may exceed 25 feet at some locations" [p12, PDF 15].
- **Zoning** `[planned]`: land stays in Agriculture district zoning until a specific subdivision or
  development proposal fits the plan [p30, PDF 33]. When the plan was written almost all of it was zoned agriculture
  `[past]` [p5, PDF 8].
- **Rustic Acres** (an existing rural subdivision of sixteen lots, `[past]` [p2, PDF 5]): the plan
  expected more low-density lots and street connections over time [p12, PDF 15]; the 2005
  amendment keeps that and adds low-medium and medium density along Milwaukee Street and at its
  corner with Sprecher Road, in small two-story buildings [PDF 67].
- **Existing non-residential uses when the plan was written** `[past]`: the Astronautics Technology Center
  (about 118 acres), a quarry of about 17 acres and construction yards on the Homburg land, the Doric
  Lodge meeting hall on Cottage Grove Road, and a Madison Gas and Electric substation near Sprecher and
  Cottage Grove Roads [p4, PDF 7].
