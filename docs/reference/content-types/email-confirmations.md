# Email Confirmations (`email-confirmations`)

- Kind: collection
- Purpose: Addresses typed into a public form, and when they were confirmed by link. Unconfirmed ones are deleted automatically 48 hours after their last link. A confirmed one is deleted automatically 12 months after the person’s last message, unless they have an account or a message on hold. To remove an address on request sooner, delete it here.
- Drafts: no
- Versions: none kept
