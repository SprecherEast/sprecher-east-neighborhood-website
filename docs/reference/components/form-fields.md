A `Form` holds `Field`s; a `Field` holds exactly one control.

## `Form`

A form (docs/ui/component-set.md): react-hook-form with a Zod schema. It shows the fixed success or error message for `status`, hides it again as soon as a field changes, and lays the fields out in a grid. The caller places the submit `Button` (`loading` while submitting).

## `FieldMoney`

A whole-dollar box (#1035): the reader types digits and the box shows them grouped by commas after a dollar sign, as they go; cents are cut. The caret stays after the digit the reader just typed or deleted, even when commas appear before it. Place it inside a `Field`.
