## `BillShare`

The whole property-tax bill at the top of a fact tab (#1018, #1111), one column on every width, in the order a neighbor needs it: the bill block's one intro line, the Rough Estimator (the home value the reader can change, which starts at the City's average home, and the switch between tax years), the answer card (the City's tax on the home and year, its increase, the year's status), the whole bill (its own card: the total, one bar whose slices are as wide as their shares, and the receipt naming each, with where the City's share goes (the split alone) as a closed row under the City's own row, #1163), then one card of two rows: why the bill may differ and the sources. The tab's other pictures (`children`) follow. Everything is drawn again for every home value and year, so every amount is the City's slice of the bill. A year in which a body has not set its rate uses that body's rate from the year before, and says so on its line in the rates panel and in the toast its Estimate button opens, so the page never changes height.

## `BillAnswer`

The answer a neighbor came for (#1111, #1118), right under the Rough Estimator. Its top row has the label on the left ("City tax on an average home", or "on your home" after an estimate) and the status badge on the right. Under it the City's tax on the home last estimated in the year chosen is the biggest text on the tab, with a small "a year", then the City's increase in the estimator's accent, orange (`increaseLine`, the same figures a tab's `{yearly}` and `{monthly}` use, so they always agree) and, while the card shows the City's average home, one small sentence with that home's value and the City's share of the whole bill. After an estimate the sentence is left out: it would only repeat the value the reader typed and a share the whole bill shows (#1183). The badge is the chosen year's stored note, with the "not final" look, and a year without a note has no badge, since its rates are final, so it can never disagree with the year. The increase line keeps its room when there is no figure, so the card never changes height. In the earliest of several years it reads "Base year" in the primary green instead, with the next year's line kept as hidden room (#1135). It draws what it is given.

## `RatesPanel`

The rates the bill is worked out from (#1351), beside the answer card: one line per body with its name and the year its rate is from on the left, and its rate as a percent with the same rate in dollars per $1,000 of value on the right. The City's line is highlighted, since the tab is about the City's tax. Every line is built the same way and as tall as the others, so the lines sit level in every year and the panel never changes height; a body with no rate keeps its line, with "No rate yet" in place of the figures. The year each rate is from is on every line, so the panel never depends on the toast (`toast`, #1380) that says why: the page puts it over the panel's top edge (the card is its positioned container), where it covers a sliver and takes no room, so the panel is as tall in every year. It draws what it is given. Its title is the same header style as the other two panels', and its status badge sits at the end of the title's line, a button that opens the toast when it has something to say (#1417).

## `BillDetails`

The two rows under the whole bill (#1111, #1118, #1163), in one card split by divider lines: why the real bill may differ (the notes, then the tab's short text and detail) and the sources, each source once, both closed when the tab loads, each titled from the block (#1158). Where the City's share goes is a row of the whole bill itself, under the City's row (`BillShare`, #1163). The sources row (the as-of line of the City's breakdown, then each link) is left out when there is neither, and a note that is not showing still takes its room, hidden from everyone, so an open row never changes height when a year is chosen. Each row is the kit's `Disclosure`, so it opens and closes without scripting and never moves what is above it.

## `RoughEstimator`

The tool at the top of a whole-bill picture (#1022): a compact orange card, named Rough Estimator for screen readers but with no title drawn, with the home value box and, on the same row at every width, the tax years as one joined two-option control of the year numbers, as tall as the box (#1118). The box has a check-mark button inside it at its end (#1110): off, flat and muted, while the typed value is not a new number, and on, accent-colored and slightly raised once it is (Enter in the box does the same), so on a phone it sits right above the keyboard (#1087). Nothing is drawn under the years: the answer card's badge says a year's status. It draws what it is given and keeps no state of its own.

## `BillFiguresProvider`

Shares each tab's City figures between its button and its heading, which sit apart in the tab list and the panel (#1029). The whole bill in a panel reports the figures for the home estimated and the year chosen; the button and the heading read them. Rendered on the server with the average home's figures, so nothing flashes wrong before scripts run.
