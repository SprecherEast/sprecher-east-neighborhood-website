## `Switch`

An on or off switch with its label beside it; pressing the label works too. For a setting that takes effect at once (a light or dark choice). A choice saved with a form goes in a `Field` (`Field.Switch`), which adds a hint and an error. Controlled (`checked`, `onCheckedChange`) or left to itself (`defaultChecked`); `name` and `value` take part in a form.

## `SwitchControl`

The track and thumb alone, for a `Field` (`Field.Switch`) that supplies the label. A native button with the switch role (Radix), so Space and Enter work and a screen reader hears "on" or "off". The track is 24 px tall; the after: area makes the target 44 px (WCAG 2.5.8). The thumb slides the other way in a right-to-left page, and neither moves under reduced motion.
