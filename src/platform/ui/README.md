# UI kit

The shared components every page and module builds with (`src/platform/ui/`): what they do beyond
their props, and which values they share with the CSS. Props, variants and where each is used:
[shared components reference](../../../docs/reference/components/README.md); rules and decided choices:
[component-set.md](../../../docs/ui/component-set.md). A change here reaches every page.

## How the pieces link

- `layoutSizes.ts` and `scrollMargin.ts` hold Tailwind classes that read CSS variables from `src/app/(frontend)/globals.css` (`--reading-width`, `--header-clearance`, `--anchor-offset`, `--chart-small-width`), so each size is defined once (#616); `layoutSizes.test.ts` and `scrollMargin.test.ts` check the names.
- `Button` and `Link` render through `AppLink`: `next/link` for routes, a plain `<a>` for in-page `#` links so keyboard focus moves (`AppLink.tsx`). `LinkButton` (`CMSLink.tsx`) turns a resolved CMS link into a `Button`: appearance `outline` becomes `secondary`, `accent` the orange `accent` button, anything else `primary`.
- `Media` picks `VideoMedia` for a `video` mime type, else `ImageMedia` (`Media.tsx`). `getMediaUrl` keeps upload paths relative so Next optimizes them as local images, and the image's `updatedAt` is appended as its cache key (`ImageMedia.client.tsx`, `src/platform/lib/getMediaUrl.ts`).
- A chart's colours follow its parts' order, `chart-1` to `chart-6`, the [theme](../theme/README.md)'s chart tokens; a chart whose spec says `palette: 'warm'` is drawn in orange and gray, by marking its root `data-chart-palette`, so no adapter changes; a seventh part throws (`src/platform/ui/chart/chartPalette.ts`), and the spec refuses it first (`src/platform/lib/chart/spec.ts`). `LegendSlot` and `SWATCH_COLOUR` in `Legend.tsx` are also fixed at six.
- A post's or an event's page heads with a plain `PageHeader` band (`variant="band"`: a themed surface under the site header, the category and the title) and shows its picture whole, never cropped, as `FeaturedImage` under the metadata bar, loading early, with its credit under it; with no picture nothing is drawn and no gap is left. The picture is full width above the text on a phone, half the column floated right from `md` and a third from `lg`, with the text wrapping beside it, and it has no Enlarge button and opens no dialog (the shared `Figure` with `zoom={false}`, so the picture starts on the text's first line, #1055). `ArticlePage` takes the picture as `image` and draws it after the metadata bar, in one box with the content that contains the float, so what follows a short text clears the picture; an article that opens with a row of cards shows them first and the picture starts beside the text after them, because a card row runs the whole column and can't sit beside a floated picture (`ArticlePage`'s `lead`, from `splitLeadingWideBlocks`); from `md` a panel or button beside it is a box of its own and takes the room left, and the text's `RichText` is given `besideFloat` so it flows beside the picture instead of staying narrow (`PageHeader.tsx`, `FeaturedImage.tsx`, `Figure.tsx`, `ArticlePage.tsx`, #1013, #1023, #1055).
- A post's or an event's metadata is the `MetaBar` above the picture: one thin line of labelled items (labels in the theme's `text-fine` size) with a hairline between them and no box, and one straight groove line (a dark hairline and the accent) directly under it across the whole column, the same at every width. An event's bar is `align="start"`: grouped at the left, from `md` one row on one text baseline, below it a two-column list above the line, and a place too long for one line cut with an ellipsis (capped at 20rem; it gives up width first, so the date stays on one line). A post's bar is `align="end"`: author and compact date only, in the `text-fine` size, one row at every width ending on the right edge, a long author name cut with an ellipsis, never wrapped. Either way the whole text of a cut value is in `title` and in the page. The line takes the width of what sits under it: a post's text, an event's panels (the whole column), and is hidden from assistive technology (`MetaBar.tsx`, `ArticlePage.tsx`'s `bylineAlign`, #1002, #1055).
- The title band can sit over a photo (`PageHeader` `variant="band"`, `backdrop`; `BandBackdrop`, #1024): one crop per screen size, phone under `md`, tablet from `md`, desktop from `lg` (the theme's breakpoints), each drawn by `Media` with `atFocalPoint`; a photo several sizes use is drawn once, and an image hidden at the current size is lazy so it is never fetched. The photo is `opacity-60` and `grayscale-50`, with a `--surface` scrim at 50% over it, so the title meets WCAG AA over any photo; the two strengths are `BAND_PHOTO_ALPHA` and `BAND_SCRIM_ALPHA` in the theme contract. It is absolutely placed inside the band, which then clips it (`relative overflow-hidden`), so the band's height does not change, and the text sits above it. A `BadgeList` with `plated` lays each badge on a solid surface plate, because a badge's tint is see-through. No crop, or an empty backdrop, draws exactly the plain band, with the same markup (`BandBackdrop.tsx`, `PageHeader.tsx`, `Badge.tsx`, `src/platform/lib/bandBackdrop.ts`).
- A picture that must stay cropped, such as the home page's hero photo, keeps its subject in view through the upload's focal point: `Media` with `atFocalPoint` sets the image's `object-position` from Payload's `focalX` and `focalY` (percent, centre when unset or out of range) through `toObjectPosition`. Cards never pass it and keep their centred crop. A focal point only chooses which part of a cropped picture shows, so it cannot keep an off-centre region such as a text panel out of every width: such a picture is shown whole (`ImageMedia.client.tsx`, `src/platform/lib/focalPoint.ts`, `PageHero.tsx`, #1013).
- `Field.Money` is a whole-dollar box: it groups the digits with commas after a dollar sign as the reader types, cuts cents (a pasted `$1,250,000.99` keeps `$1,250,000`), and keeps the caret after the digit just typed or deleted even when commas appear before it (`FieldMoney.client.tsx`, `lib/money.ts`, #1035).
- `Field` in the `bar` layout puts its `action` beside the box, or inside it with `actionPlacement="inside"` (#1110): the box draws the one border and the one focus ring, the action sits at the trailing end with the same 6 px inset above, below and at the end, and the border takes the accent while the action is not disabled. The check-mark inside it is a `Button` of the `action` variant (flat and muted while disabled, accent-colored and lifted by a shadow while enabled, no lift with reduced motion) and the `icon-sm` size, drawn 36 px and tapped as 44 px (`Field.client.tsx`, `Button.tsx`).
- A `Field` hands its one control its id, `aria-describedby` and `aria-invalid` through one context; a control outside a `Field` throws (`fieldContext.ts`, `Field.client.tsx`).

## Behaviors

- A button with `loading` shows a spinner and ignores clicks but stays focusable (`aria-disabled`), so focus isn't lost (`Button.tsx`). `external` opens a new tab and adds spoken text saying so (`Button.tsx`, `Link.tsx`).
- Charts: `Chart` checks the spec, asks `chart/chartRegistry.ts` for an adapter, and draws the key, caption and numbers table around it. The first adapter that supports the kind draws it (`breakdown`: breakdown; `css`: units; `visx`: bar, stacked); the `text` adapter supports every kind and is last. Every adapter passes `chart/chartAdapter.contract.tsx`; chart libraries are allowed only under `chart/adapters/` (lint, `CHART_LIBRARIES` in `eslint.boundaries.config.mjs`).
- A breakdown (#866) is drawn at full width, not as a thumbnail: a grid of squares worth a fixed dollar amount each (rounded to whole squares, largest remainder; `hideSquares` leaves out the grid, its key and the square value, so only the ranked bars show, #943), a key in dollars, ranked bars with the main pieces nested under their area in the area's colour, and the other pieces in one list under "See the smaller pieces", biggest first and coloured by their own rank. Colour follows size, from `chart-ramp-1` to `chart-ramp-7`; pale steps keep a thin outline and every figure is also text. Bar length is share times one shared scale, so a dollar is the same length in every breakdown (`chart/adapters/breakdown/`, `lib/chart/breakdown.ts`; the design reference is `docs/ui/reference/breakdown-chart-preview.html`).
- Every chart carries its numbers as a real table under "Show the numbers", closed by default, or for screen readers only when the chart is given `numbers="screen-reader"`, as in a fact tab (`chart/ChartDataTable.tsx`, #980). A chart can also leave its title and takeaway to screen readers (`heading="screen-reader"`), and draws no caption when it has nothing to say, so no gap is left. A drawing that gains from size is a thumbnail; `ChartZoom` is a thin caller of the shared `Zoom`, which moves the same server-rendered chart into a full-screen `Dialog` instead of copying it, and is the chart's only client code (`chart/ChartZoom.client.tsx`, `Zoom.client.tsx`). With scripting off the Enlarge button is hidden.
- `Figure` floats a small picture right from `md` (two fifths of the text column by default; `size` sets a third or half; `half-then-third` is the article picture's, #1023) and fills the column below. Its key, long caption and the larger picture show only in the `Zoom` dialog, never in the small view (`Figure.tsx`, `Zoom.client.tsx`, #816).
- `VersionCheck` (root layout) polls `/api/version` every 30 s, but only while the reader has been active in the last 30 s and the tab is visible, and again when the tab regains focus. The first answer is the baseline; a different build shows a toast with a Refresh button that reloads. A dismissed build isn't offered again; a newer one is. It does nothing when the server reports `development` (`VersionCheck.client.tsx`, `src/app/(frontend)/api/version/route.ts`).
- `AdminBar` is always mounted but hidden until Payload's bar reports a signed-in admin or editor (`isStaffRole`); residents see nothing (`AdminBar.client.tsx`, #203). `LivePreviewListener` refreshes the page when the editor saves in Live Preview; `[slug]/page.tsx` mounts it only in draft mode.
- `LoadMore` (`useLoadMore`, `LoadMoreFooter`) loads the next page by cursor shortly before the end, for 4 pages counting the first (`AUTO_LOAD_PAGES`); after that the reader clicks "Load more", so the footer stays reachable. The button is a real link to the next numbered page, so it works without JavaScript and in a new tab; a repeated item is dropped; if focus was on the button when the last load removed it, focus moves to the status line (`LoadMore.client.tsx`).
- The "On this page" menu needs at least 3 sections (`MIN_SECTIONS` in `onThisPage.ts`); the highlighted section is the last heading above a reading line taken from the heading's own scroll margin plus a little slack, so the heading a click lands on is the highlighted one.
- `MapEmbed` draws a live map only when `toGoogleMapsEmbedUrl` accepts the address (an `https` Google embed address). The frame is created only once the dialog opens, and with no valid address only the picture and text show. It fills the width it is given (`MapEmbed.client.tsx`, `src/platform/lib/googleMapsEmbed.ts`).
- `SidebarCTA` draws its sentence only when given one, with the heading's gap to the button widened to match, so a box whose heading and button already say it has no empty line (`SidebarCTA.tsx`, #1010).
- `Section` `placement="inline"` draws only its tone, with no container or padding, for a block inside a page section's cell (`Section.tsx`, #884).
- A `MetaList` value can shrink inside its row, so a clamped or truncated value stays on its line instead of widening the list (`MetaList.tsx`).
- `FORM_MESSAGES` are the fixed texts for a failed send ("offline", "failed"); each form adds only its own cases (`formMessages.ts`, #204).
- `ShareBar` draws one whole as a flat bar of slices as wide as their shares, in one SVG whose widths are percent attributes (never an inline style), so it needs no measuring and draws the same on the server. The slices keep the order given, in the tones of `shareTones.ts` (the highlighted slice in the theme's green, three grays, the lightest first; the receipt's small squares use the same table, so the two never differ). A slice at least `SHARE_BAR_INSIDE_MIN` (8%) wide writes its label in it; a narrower one leaves it to the text beside the bar. The caller writes the label for screen readers (`ShareBar.tsx`, #1018, #1111).
- Motion helpers (#1048, #1068): `prefersReducedMotion()` in `motion.client.ts`, and `useCountedNumber(target)`, which counts the shown number to a new target in 1.5 seconds (`COUNT_MS`; `YEAR_COUNT_MS`, half a second, is for a switch of tax year, #1087) and jumps at once with reduced motion.
- `ShareBill` draws one whole as a bill: the total on top (it flashes the accent once each time `pulseKey` changes, #1051), one `ShareBar` made from the slices, and under it a receipt that names every slice with its color, percent and dollars, in the bar's own order because both come from the same slices. The receipt is one grid of three columns, the name and two right-aligned columns of fixed width (percent, amount) kept on one line, so each column lines up from row to row and the block keeps its height when the amounts change. The caller writes the label for screen readers (`ShareBill.tsx`, #1111).
- `Tabs` gives every tab a link of its own (#1097), on for every tabbed block with no setting: choosing a tab puts `#<anchor>` in the address (the item's `anchor`, else its `value`), replacing the history entry so Back leaves the page; opening an address that names a tab, or the address changing on the page, opens that tab and scrolls the tabs into view below the header; a name that is no tab changes nothing, and the first tab is shown. A tabbed block whose names repeat those of an earlier block on the page numbers them `-2`, `-3`, so every tab on a page has a link of its own.
- `AmountBar` draws one amount as a single bar of parts as wide as their shares, with the title and whole amount above it and a key under it only when there is more than one part; a `hatch` part is the striped increase. The caller formats the amounts (`AmountBar.tsx`, #1020).
- `ScaledBreakdown` draws a breakdown chart in the browser with every amount multiplied by a factor, from the same drawing code as `Chart`'s breakdown, so the City's tax split follows the home value a reader types. It takes a spec the server already checked and never parses it, so no chart library and no spec rules (zod) reach the browser (`chart/ScaledBreakdown.tsx`, `lib/chart/scale.ts`, #1020). `ChartCaption` is the caption under a chart, shared by both.
- `ProgressBar` is a native `<progress>` with a required `label` for screen readers; its fill comes from the element's own value (no inline style), a value is kept between 0 and `max`, and a `max` of 0 shows an empty bar. Callers always write the numbers beside it (`ProgressBar.tsx`, #939).
- `Dialog` is a full-screen `sheet` by default; `placement="center"` is a panel over a dimmed page. `RouteDialog` is an always-open centered `Dialog` for an intercepted route: its close button, back button and Escape all call `router.back()` (`Dialog.client.tsx`, `RouteDialog.client.tsx`, #946).
- `Badge` has `open` (green) and `done` (purple) marks, each with words and an icon from the caller, and `outline` for a plain label (`Badge.tsx`, #946). `size` is `sm` (the default) or `md`, with larger words and icon; the plan's Open, Done and Not planned marks use `md` and its labels stay small (#952).
- `Alert` announces itself unless `live={false}`: errors interrupt, other tones wait their turn (`Alert.tsx`); the `info` tone is blue, from the `info` and `info-strong` theme tokens (#818).

## Shared pieces it uses

- Related notes: [theme](../theme/README.md) (chart tokens; its `ThemeSelector` uses `Field`), [rich text](../richtext/README.md) (renders charts and blocks with the kit, and the "On this page" menu), [shared Payload code](../payload/README.md) (`AdminBar` reads `isStaffRole`).
- Helpers from `src/platform/lib/` (`cn`, `getMediaUrl`, chart `spec`/`table`, `googleMapsEmbed`, `getURL`) and `src/platform/payload/roles.ts`; `@/cssVariables` and `@/imageQualities` for images.

## Rules and the tests that protect them

- Pages and modules use the kit, never raw `a`, `button`, `input`, `select`, `textarea`, `details`, `summary` or `img`; the native element lives inside a kit component (guard rail 2). Lint bans them outside `src/platform/ui/` (`eslint.ui-kit.config.mjs`).
- `Disclosure` has four looks: `card`, `row` (the card with no vertical padding on the box, so a closed row is only as tall as its summary and a few stack compactly, #1111), `bar` and `link`. A `Disclosure` chevron turns only for its own box, so boxes can nest: `Disclosure.test.tsx` runs the real Tailwind output against a nested and a top-level box (#967).
- Sizes and scroll margins read one variable each: `layoutSizes.test.ts`, `scrollMargin.test.ts`.
- The registry's order and the text fallback: `chart/chartRegistry.test.ts`; every adapter's markup (no `style`, no colour literals, real text, axe): `chart/chartAdapter.contract.test.tsx`.
- Chart libraries stay in the adapters: `chart/chartLibraries.lint.test.ts`; the browser never gets a library or the spec rules through the zoom: `chart/ChartZoom.imports.test.ts`.
- Chart colours, the six-part limit and the seven-step ramp: `chart/chartPalette.test.ts`; the breakdown: `chart/adapters/breakdown/breakdownAdapter.test.tsx`.
- Each component's behavior and accessibility: its `*.test.tsx` beside it (`Button`, `Link`, `Field.client`, `LoadMore.client`, `MapEmbed.client`, `AdminBar.client`, `OnThisPage.client`, `Dialog.client`, `Toast`, `ProgressBar`, `RouteDialog.client` and others).

## Boundaries

- It imports only from `src/platform/` and no module (0008; no file here imports `@/modules`). `index.ts` exports the small primitives; larger ones are imported by file so a client component that needs a Button doesn't bundle them (`index.ts`).
